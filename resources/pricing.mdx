Pipedream offers a generous free tier with no credit card required. As your usage grows, paid plans unlock higher limits, team collaboration features, and priority support.

<Note>
  For current pricing details and plan comparisons, visit [pipedream.com/pricing](https://pipedream.com/pricing).
</Note>

## What are credits?

Credits are Pipedream's compute unit. Each time a workflow step runs, it consumes a credit. The number of credits you have per day determines how many workflow executions you can run.

- One credit is consumed per step execution
- Triggers do not consume credits
- Credits reset daily
- Unused credits do not roll over

## Plan overview

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free (Developer)">

    **Included:**
    - No credit card required
    - Access to 1,000+ app integrations

    - Longer step execution timeouts
    - Increased workflow concurrency
    - Team workspaces and shared credentials
    - Priority support
    - SSO and advanced security controls (higher tiers)

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Frequently asked billing questions

**Do I need a credit card to start?**
No. The free tier requires no credit card.

**What happens if I exceed my daily credit quota?**
Workflow executions will be paused until the next day's quota resets, or you can upgrade to a paid plan.

**Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?**
Yes. Plan changes take effect immediately.

**Is there a free trial for paid plans?**
Visit [pipedream.com/pricing](https://pipedream.com/pricing) for current trial and promotional details.
